David is the Academy Manager at DevScope in Viseu, Portugal, working in the Microsoft ecosystem. He began his career at Microsoft Lisbon and later led development teams at Bizdirect/Sonae IM and DevScope (twice).

He holds a degree in Computer Engineering from UBI and a master's from IPV Viseu. Currently, David is researching Chain‑of‑Thought reasoning in large language models.

At heart he's a researcher and a curious mind — interested in politics, cognitive science and how the mind works. He maintains a regular meditation and mindfulness practice.

When not researching or mentoring, David tinkers with home automation, runs local LLMs and connects smart sensors around his house. He also rides cross‑country mountain bikes to clear his head.

Projects include "motaDB" and several automation/backup tools — the code is available in this repository.
