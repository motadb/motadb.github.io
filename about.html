Academy Manager at DevScope in Viseu, Portugal, working in the Microsoft ecosystem. He began his career at Microsoft Lisbon and later led development teams at Bizdirect/Sonae IM before returning to DevScope.

He holds a degree in Computer Engineering from UBI and a master's from IPV Viseu. Currently researching Chain-of-Thought reasoning in large language models.

At heart a researcher and a curious mind — interested in politics, cognitive science, and how the mind works. He maintains a regular meditation and mindfulness practice.

When not researching or mentoring, David tinkers with home automation, runs local LLMs, and connects smart sensors around his house. He also rides cross-country mountain bikes to clear his head.
